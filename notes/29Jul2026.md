### Implementation Tip During Phase 2 & 3

When ingesting $e$ (TKE / turbulent energy proxy) and $Ri$ from observational datasets vs. numerical SCM payloads:

* **Floor & Scale Guard:** Ensure observational $e$ values are clipped at the physical laminar floor ($e_{\text{floor}} \approx 10^{-3} \text{ m}^2\text{s}^{-2}$) before calculating the $\eta^*$ projection.
* **Gradient Regularization:** With real observational data, sampling intervals $dt$ can occasionally be irregular or noisy. The 5-point Savitzky-Golay derivative filter in V4 will handle uniform $dt$ smoothly, but ensure timestamps are strictly monotonically increasing during input validation.
