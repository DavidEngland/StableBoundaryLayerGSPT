## Technical Architectural Strengths

### 1. Singular Geometry & Manifold Regularization

* **Hyperbolic Embedding ($\xi$):** The discriminant $D = \beta_{\text{gspt}}^2 + 4\Delta$ uses a hyperbolic embedding $\sqrt{0.5(D + \sqrt{D^2 + \xi^2})}$ to eliminate the square-root singularity at the fold threshold ($D \to 0$), keeping the RHS $C^\infty$-smooth across state transitions.
* **Lipschitz Activation Gate ($\alpha_{\text{gate}}$):** The regularized gate $\psi = \frac{\sqrt{\tilde{e}}}{\sqrt{\tilde{e}} + \alpha_{\text{gate}}}$ prevents non-differentiable corner cases when TKE/mixing scales approach zero.
* **Bounded Stability Response:** The bounded $\tanh$ formulation in `_bounded_stability_response` avoids exponential runaway when dealing with strong non-local thermal stratification.

### 2. Physical & Diagnostics Safety

* **`SurfaceAnomalyException`:** Rich exception context captures critical physical states ($T_s$, $T_{\text{rad}}$, $\theta_1$, $\vert{}V\vert{}$) at failure, making debugging radiative cooling runaway or numerical decoupling straightforward.
* **Bulk-Transfer Surface Energy Balance:** Computing $C_H$ via logarithmic roughness scales ($z_{0m}, z_{0h}$) rather than treating $K_h$ as a raw exchange coefficient ensures surface flux scaling remains physically consistent across grid resolutions.

## Project Overview

This project develops a mathematically rigorous, numerically stable **Single Column Model (SCM)** and dynamic solver architecture for characterizing transitions in the nocturnal **Stable Boundary Layer (SBL)**.

---

## 1. Core Theoretical Foundations (GSPT Architecture)

* **Fast-Slow Scale Separation:** The SBL is cast as a fast-slow dynamical system governed by a singular perturbation parameter $0 < \epsilon \ll 1$. Turbulent kinetic energy (TKE, $e$) acts as the fast variable that rapidly relaxes toward equilibrium, while wind momentum $(U, V)$, potential temperature ($\theta$), and skin temperature ($T_s$) act as slow macroscale variables.
* **Critical Manifold Geometry ($\mathcal{M}_0$):** The system state relaxes onto an invariant critical manifold featuring an active turbulent sheet (a shear-stratification paraboloid) and a stable laminar floor sheet ($e = -\delta$).
* **Catastrophe Separation:** The model explicitly separates physical boundary crossings ($e = 0$, where transport shifts to residual background mixing) from non-degenerate fold catastrophes ($\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}}$), where normal hyperbolicity breaks down and slow flows jump along fast fibers, causing "brittle" turbulence collapse.

---

## 2. Regularization & Numerical Engineering

* **$C^\infty$ Hyperbolic Regularization:** Instead of non-differentiable hard clipping (e.g., `max(0, e)`), the fast vector field utilizes smooth hyperbolic embeddings and safeguard gates to preserve differentiability across the entire state space.
* **Tri-Fold Role of Background Mixing ($\delta$):**
* *Mathematical:* Preserves normal hyperbolicity for Fenichel's Theorem.
* *Physical:* Models unresolved background mixing (internal gravity waves, terrain drainage).
* *Numerical:* Bounds vertical diffusivities ($K_m, K_h$) away from zero, eliminating division-by-zero singularities.


* **High-Performance Implicit Solver:** Uses L-stable implicit integrators (specifically `Rodas5P`) combined with Automatic Differentiation (AD) and banded Jacobian matrix structures (`BandedMatrix`), reducing $N$-node vertical linear solver evaluations from $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ to $\mathcal{O}(1)$ execution cost.

## 3. Emergent Dynamics & Tri-Height Tracking

* **Dynamic Boundary Layer Emergence:** SBL height is not hardcoded or prescribed. It emerges dynamically via a slow-manifold occupancy function $\phi(e)$.
* **Inertial Oscillations & Low-Level Jets (LLJ):** When turbulence collapses, upper-level momentum decoupling spontaneously generates Blackadar-type inertial oscillations tracing circular hodographs.
* **Tri-Height Geometric Diagnostics:** Tracks SBL detachment using triple-point convergence across three complementary metrics:
1. *Decoupling Height ($h_D$):* Manifold occupancy drops below 5%.
2. *Energetic Collapse Height ($h_e$):* TKE drops below collapse threshold.
